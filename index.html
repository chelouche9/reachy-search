🔎 Reachy Search

Hold something up. Boop an antenna. Ask.

Reachy looks at the object, spins its antennas while it searches the web, and tells you the answer out loud.

How it works

  1. Boop an antenna

    The antennas run at low gain, so they double as physical buttons. Reachy grabs a camera frame the instant you touch one.

  2. Ask out loud

    It records until you stop talking. Speech recognition runs locally — your microphone audio never leaves the machine.

  3. It thinks, visibly

    Claude identifies the object and writes a search query, Tavily searches, Claude writes the reply. Meanwhile Reachy plays one of three thinking animations, picked at random.

  4. It answers

    Spoken through the robot's own speaker, with the head moving along to the voice.

“What is this?”

That's a stovetop moka pot. Water boils in the bottom chamber and pressure pushes it up through the coffee. Yours looks like a six-cup aluminium one.

Ask it anything you'd ask while holding the thing — is this dishwasher safe, find me a cheaper one, what do I even do with this.

The thinking moves

The latency isn't hidden. The animation is the interface. Three of them, chosen at random per search.

ponder

Head cocks to one side and holds, sweeping slowly like it's reading a shelf. Antennas counter-rotate, a dial being turned.

scan

A small figure-eight, antennas flicking alternately, deliberately off the head's rhythm.

spin_up

The literal loading spinner — antennas counter-rotate, accelerating from a lazy turn to a blur.

Setup

Two API keys, both with usable free tiers. Install from the Reachy Mini dashboard, open the app's settings page, paste them in. That's the whole configuration.

  • Anthropic — sees the object, writes the answer
  • Tavily — searches the web

Speech recognition and synthesis are local, so there's nothing else to sign up for.
